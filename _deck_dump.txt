=== Slide 2 ===
WHY PAWFINDER
Adoption in Coimbatore is broken
Thousands of street and shelter dogs wait for homes — but the path from “I want to adopt” to a dog at home is scattered, offline, and opaque.
🔍
Discovery is hard
No single place to browse dogs across local shelters with real filters.
📋
The process is opaque
Paper forms and phone calls — applicants never know where they stand.
🤝
Help is fragmented
Fostering and volunteering run on informal, untracked WhatsApp threads.
✅
The fix  One local hub where adopters, fosters and volunteers meet verified Coimbatore shelters — and every step is tracked.
