===== SLIDE 1 =====
PawFinder | A dog adoption, foster & volunteer platform — built for Coimbatore | Express  ·  better-sqlite3  ·  Vanilla-JS SPA  ·  Electron desktop app | PROJECT OVERVIEW  ·  15 SLIDES

===== SLIDE 2 =====
WHY THIS PROJECT | The problem PawFinder solves | Coimbatore has thousands of street and shelter dogs waiting for homes — but the path from “I want to adopt” to a dog in your home is broken. | Rescue information is scattered across individual shelters, WhatsApp groups and phone calls. Adoption is paperwork-heavy, opaque, and offline. | THE VISION | One trusted, local hub where adopters, fosters and volunteers meet verified Coimbatore shelters — and every step is tracked. | Discovery is hard | No single place to browse dogs across local shelters with real filters. | Process is opaque | Paper forms, no status, no idea where an adoption application stands. | Help is fragmented | Fostering and volunteering rely on informal, untracked coordination. | 2 | PawFinder

===== SLIDE 3 =====
PRODUCT AT A GLANCE | One platform, five journeys | PawFinder is a single-page web app (packaged as a Windows desktop app) that connects people to dogs through five integrated flows. | Adopt | Browse, search & filter dogs; rich health & temperament profiles; favourites. | Apply | Multi-step adoption application with document uploads & live status. | Foster | Short-term foster listings flagged by urgency and shelter need. | Volunteer | Roles, events, a paw-points leaderboard and community drives. | Shelters | Directory of 8 verified Coimbatore shelters with stats & messaging. | Admin | Console to manage dogs, shelters and review applications. | 3 | PawFinder

===== SLIDE 4 =====
ENGINEERING | Technology stack | Desktop shell | Electron 33 + electron-builder — runs the Express server in-process, single window, single-instance lock. | Backend | Node.js + Express 4 — 9 modular REST routers, session middleware, SPA fallback. | Data layer | better-sqlite3 (SQLite, WAL mode, foreign keys) — synchronous, file-based, zero-config. | Frontend | Vanilla-JS single-page app — no framework: hand-rolled router, ~1,670 lines of app.js. | Auth & uploads | express-session + bcryptjs for auth; multer for document uploads. | CODEBASE | ~5,100 | lines of code | 12 | SQLite tables | 9 | API route modules | ~40 | REST endpoints | 4 | PawFinder

===== SLIDE 5 =====
ARCHITECTURE | How a request flows | Electron Shell | BrowserWindow | loads localhost:38217 | SPA Client | index.html · app.js | fetch() to /api/* | Express Server | sessions · static | 9 REST routers | better-sqlite3 | pawfinder.db (WAL) | prepared statements | DESIGN NOTES | Server-in-process — Electron starts the same Express app used in dev (no separate API process). | Data & uploads live in Electron userData (writable per-user dir) via PAWFINDER_DB / PAWFINDER_UPLOADS. | Catalogue content re-syncs idempotently on every boot — INSERT OR IGNORE keeps user data intact. | 5 | PawFinder

===== SLIDE 6 =====
BACKEND | API surface — 9 REST routers | Router | Mounted at | Responsibility | auth | /api/auth | Register, login, logout, current-user session | dogs | /api/dogs | Browse/search/filter, featured & urgent, favourites, admin CRUD | shelters | /api/shelters | Directory, per-shelter stats, admin CRUD | applications | /api/applications | Submit application + document uploads, admin decisions | users | /api/users | Profile, favourites list, notifications | articles | /api/articles | Pet-care knowledge content | foster | /api/foster | Foster dog listings & program stats | volunteer | /api/volunteer | Roles, events, leaderboard, stats | chat | /api/chat | User ↔ shelter messaging threads | Access is gated by two session-based guards: requireAuth (any logged-in user) and requireAdmin (admin only). | 6 | PawFinder

===== SLIDE 9 =====
FEATURE 2 — APPLY | A tracked adoption application | STEP 1 | Application form | Residence, outdoor space, experience, other pets, children, hours alone, reason. | STEP 2 | Document upload | Gov ID, address & income proof, home photos — multer, 8 MB, JPG/PNG/WEBP/PDF. | STEP 3 | Review queue | Status starts pending with a progress %; lands in the admin review queue. | STEP 4 | Admin decision | Admin approves or declines; applicant sees the updated status live. | Why it matters:   | the applicant always knows where they stand, and shelters get structured, document-backed requests instead of scattered phone calls — uploads are validated for type and size, and stored in a writable per-user directory. | 9 | PawFinder

OPERATIONS | Admin & access control | Two session-based middleware guards split the app into a public/member surface and an admin console. | requireAuth | Any logged-in member | Browse & favourite dogs | Submit adoption applications + documents | Message shelters, manage profile | View notifications & paw-points | requireAdmin | Admin console | Full CRUD on dogs & shelters | See every application + uploaded documents | Approve / decline with one action | Seeded demo admin: demo@pawfinder.in | 12 | PawFinder

===== SLIDE 13 =====
HARDENING | Security posture | Hashed passwords | Passwords stored as bcrypt hashes (bcryptjs) — never in plain text. | Parameterised SQL | Every query is a prepared statement — structurally safe from SQL injection. | Session auth | HTTP-only session cookies (7-day), role checks on every protected route. | Upload validation | multer enforces MIME allow-list (images + PDF) and an 8 MB / file cap. | Known hardening TODO:   | the Express session secret is currently hardcoded and flagged “change-in-production” — it should move to an environment variable / secret before any real deployment. | 13 | PawFinder

===== SLIDE 14 =====
DELIVERY | Shipped as a Windows desktop app | Electron shell | electron-main.js boots the Express server in-process on port 38217 and shows it in one BrowserWindow with a single-instance lock. | Writable data dir | DB + uploads are redirected to Electron userData so they survive an app that ships read-only inside app.asar. | Build pipeline | npm run prebuild:electron fetches a better-sqlite3 build for Electron, then electron-builder --win produces the artifact. | BUILD REALITY | Project path contains a space, and the machine runs Node 24 with no better-sqlite3 prebuilts — so native modules need careful, documented juggling. | The shipped artifact is a zip of dist/win-unpacked/ — a self-contained PawFinder.exe (a single-file installer isn’t producible in this environment). | 14 | PawFinder
